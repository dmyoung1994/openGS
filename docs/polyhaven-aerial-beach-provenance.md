The maritime backdrop uses the original 2K JPEG diffuse, OpenGL normal, and
roughness maps without generated or procedural replacement imagery. The shader
projects the maps in world space at the declared 30 m source width and combines a
second rotated, incommensurate projection to prevent a visible square repeat over
the larger coastal apron. Biome weights determine where the material appears;
gameplay surface and collision classification remain unchanged.
