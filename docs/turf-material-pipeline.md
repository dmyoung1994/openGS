# Maintained turf material pipeline

Fairway, green, fringe, and tee remain a material/heightfield system. There is no
short-blade geometry in this path; geometry is reserved for the authored rough and
deep-rough system in `Grass.js`.

## Runtime frequency hierarchy

`Terrain.js` combines three physically different scales:

1. The Blendkit fairway/green NRH map supplies shoot-scale normal and canopy height.
2. Its explicit texture LOD, bounded parallax march, and real-world tile size remove
   unresolved source motifs instead of repeating them into a close-up stamp.
3. The GPU-generated macro field supplies world-anchored phase, colour, and moisture
   bands. It now generates mipmaps after its one-time compute write, so the same field
   remains stable when a pixel covers many 0.5 m authoring texels.

The maintained normal path uses filtered macro-channel gradients for broad fibre and
meso response. It does not evaluate a full-screen procedural noise gradient per
fragment. The mower pass remains a linear world-space coordinate, so its frequency and
direction stay registered with the normal, roughness, and pigment responses.

## Close-up stability

Parallax is limited to 2.5 source texels of total travel and falls back at grazing view
angles. The sun-directed canopy check is limited to 2.0 source texels. These bounds keep
the heightfield from smearing across unrelated shoots when the camera is nearly tangent
to the ground.

The final world-space normal is differentiated once for a bounded normal-variance
estimate. That estimate increases matte roughness and lowers the specular peak together,
which is a screen-space specular anti-aliasing response rather than a painted darkening.
All changes are render-only; collision height, ball lie, zone ownership, and course
geometry remain authoritative.

## Blendkit provenance and reproduction

The source URLs, asset/version IDs, license note, hashes, bake dimensions, and runtime
channel layout are recorded in [blendkit-turf-provenance.md](blendkit-turf-provenance.md).
The checked-in derivatives are not regenerated by ordinary builds. Validate them with:

```sh
python3 scripts/validate_turf_pipeline.py
```

The validator is read-only. It verifies four 2048² RGBA derivatives, their SHA-256
hashes, and the required provenance markers. Re-baking remains an explicit offline
operation through the pinned Blender and packing commands in the provenance record.
