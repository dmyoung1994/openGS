# Vibe recipes

Choose a primary biome, then tune density and finish. Use compatible-biome

## Desert

- Hero trees: `desert-mesquite`, `blue-palo-verde`, `desert-willow`; reserve
  `desert-fan-palm` for water, resort, or oasis cues.
- Middle story: `creosote-bush`, `great-basin-sagebrush`, `jojoba`.
- Accents: `brittlebush`, `desert-marigold`, `firecracker-penstemon`,
  `prickly-pear-patch`, `utah-agave`.
- Geology: `red-sandstone-boulder`, `sandstone-strata-stack`,
  `desert-basalt-boulder`, `desert-weathered-snag`.
- Premium desert drama: sparse trees, clustered shrubs, large negative spaces,
  and 2–4 coherent rock outcrops that frame—not cover—the route.

## Parkland

- Hero trees: `parkland-white-oak`, `parkland-red-maple`,
  `parkland-american-elm`, `parkland-river-birch`.
- Middle story: `parkland-rhododendron`, `parkland-hydrangea`,
  `parkland-boxwood`.
- Ground layer: `parkland-fescue-clump`, `parkland-wood-fern`,
  `parkland-clover-mat`, `parkland-wildflower-mix`.
- Punctuation: `parkland-granite-boulder`, `parkland-limestone-cluster`,
  `parkland-fallen-oak`, `parkland-hollow-stump`, `parkland-branch-pile`.
- Tournament parkland: clean trunks and understory near play, naturalized outer
  woodland, framed green backdrop, and restrained flowering masses.

- Middle story: `mountain-greenleaf-manzanita`, `mountain-mahogany`,

## Grasslands — naturalized golf

Visual reference: [user-supplied golf landscape photograph](grasslands-reference.jpeg),
provided September 4, 2026. Inspect it before composing this style. It is a design
reference, not a licensed runtime texture or permission to redistribute outside
this project. Location, species, photographer, and capture time are unverified.

- **Composition:** broad, connected native-grass masses frame precisely maintained
  tee and fairway islands. Preserve open views and rolling landform legibility;
  trees are occasional anchors and distant groups, not a continuous perimeter wall.
  Follow contours with irregular drifts, setbacks, and openings rather than
  isolated tufts evenly scattered across the entire site.
- **Palette:** muted deep-green maintained turf against olive-green basal foliage,
  tan/straw stems, and restrained gray-brown seedheads. Keep green and senescent
  plants mixed within a stand. Avoid uniformly bright yellow rough, neon fairways,
  and identical color noise on every clump. Warmth in the photo comes partly from
  the sky; do not bake sunset lighting into albedo.
- **Structure:** the foreground reads as dense, slender, upright stems with arching
  blades and fine seedheads above a darker tangled base. Vary height, density,
  orientation, and maturity in coherent patches, with occasional taller stems.
  Judge physical scale against tees and nearby objects; do not mistake every tall
  grass for reeds or claim a precise species from this photograph.
- **Mowing transitions:** retain crisp, correctly registered playable turf edges,
  a short green rough band, then increasing height and straw content into native
  grass. Ecological irregularity belongs outside the maintained cut; it is not
  a blurred material boundary or permission to change surface IDs and ball lies.
- **Depth and motion:** close views need convincing stem/seedhead silhouettes;
  middle views should merge into layered clumps and distant views into continuous
  grassland masses. Shared wind should bend neighboring stems coherently, with
  subtler seedhead flutter and planted roots. Avoid synchronized whole-field sway,
  popping bare ground, or thinning that erases the distant mass.
- **Source selection:** inspect the current catalog for suitable upright native
  grass/groundcover roles and audition source geometry, alpha, normals, and PBR in
  the production renderer. Do not invent catalog IDs or scale lawn grass into tall
  native stands. If the required silhouette is missing, record the asset gap and
  follow the skill's acquisition rules. Route missing renderer capabilities to
  engine work; course records must not smuggle in shader or physics changes.
- **Acceptance:** compare a low foreground view, golfer-height tee/approach, and
  elevated overview to the reference's maintained/native contrast and layered
  density. Also inspect daylight and live wind: sunset alone can conceal weak
  material response. Use the existing atmosphere timeline for an optional warm
  evening presentation; do not copy the photograph's buildings or layout unless
  separately requested.

## Cross-biome style controls

- Realistic: 5–7 IDs, restrained density, ecology-led transitions.
- Hybrid: 6–9 IDs, realistic communities plus one exaggerated signature frame.
- Spectacle: retain one biome grammar, amplify silhouette and contrast, and
  keep physics and shot choices authentic.
- Manicured: fewer deadwood pieces, clean turf margins, designed shrub masses.
- Naturalized: irregular multi-age groups, more groundcover and deadwood, soft
  transition bands.
- Sparse: use 35–55% of balanced counts and enlarge negative-space corridors.
- Lush: use 130–170% of balanced understory counts, never tree counts, while
  preserving the same protected gameplay zones.
